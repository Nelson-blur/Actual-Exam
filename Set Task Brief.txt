You work for a software development company. The company has secured a new contract to develop a digital solution for Greenfield Local Hub (GLH). GLH is a cooperative of local farmers and food producers. GLH offers:

1. A single location retail outlet for locally produced food and drink
2. Collective marketing for local farmers and food producers.
3. Product management, stock control and product Traceability.

GLH (the client) would like to develop a digital solution that will:
1. Provide information about the producers, their methods and the benefits of buying locally
2. Allow customers to:
   - View products with transparent pricing and availability
   - Place orders for collection and delivery
   - Manage their accounts and view order history
3. Provide a dashboard to enable producers to manage stock levels, update product d4etails and view orders.

The Client has conducted market research with existing customers to identify features that could be included in the digital solution. The features suggested by the client are:
1. Order management, scheduling and Tracking features
2. A loyalty scheme that provides discounts and special offers 
3. Accessibility features to support a wide range of users.
